Questions

Frequently asked questions

If you're new to hiring a grant writer, you're in good company — here are the things nonprofits ask most.

What exactly does a grant writer do?

I find grant opportunities that fit your mission, write the proposals that funders want to see, and help with the reporting after you win. Think of it as borrowing an experienced development staffer — without the full-time hire.

How much does it cost?

Every nonprofit is different, so I scope each engagement to your needs and budget. The first consultation is always free — we'll talk through options before you commit to anything.

What happens in the free consultation?

It's a relaxed listening session — usually a phone or video call. You share your funding goals, what you've tried, and what's worked, and I'll tell you honestly where I can help. No pressure and no pitch.

Do we have to be a 501(c)(3)?

Most funders require it, but not all — and "pending" status is fine for a conversation. Tell me where you are on the contact form and we'll figure out the best path together.

Can you guarantee we'll win a grant?

No one honestly can — funding is competitive. What I can promise is well-researched, well-written, on-time proposals that give you a real shot. My grants have succeeded about half the time, which is strong for this field.

Do you work with organizations outside Wisconsin?

Yes — I work with nonprofits remotely, so location isn't a barrier. Let's talk about your goals on a free consultation.
