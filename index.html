What I Do

Three ways I help you win funding

Hands-on support across the full grant lifecycle — so your team can stay focused on your mission.

🔍

Grant Research & Opportunity Identification

I identify grant opportunities aligned with your mission and budget. Instead of spending weeks hunting through databases, you get a curated list of realistic funding sources — community foundations, humanities councils, state grants — matched to your sector and capacity. You'll know exactly where to apply.

✍️

Grant Proposal Writing

I write competitive, funder-focused proposals tailored to each funder's priorities and requirements. I handle the research, drafting, and revisions so your team stays focused on your mission.

📋

Post-Award Reporting & Compliance

Winning a grant comes with reporting obligations. I help manage compliance documentation, progress reports, and impact statements so you stay in good standing with funders — and keep the door open for future funding.
